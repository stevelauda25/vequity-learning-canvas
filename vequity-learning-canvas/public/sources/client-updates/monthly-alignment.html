Monthly publication alignment

Discussion recorded 25 September 2026 · Original client artifacts remain unchanged.

Client direction

The user supplied Steve’s message and screenshot after a client call:

So the report that being generated/send out to users will be every first date in every month.

Steve requested brainstorming on the implications across the app. The earlier JSON for Design note, 21 September, §4 already names the next first of the month; it records API readiness only as of that date.

Existing PRD requirements

  • First reports are operator-triggered. Before a report exists, show being prepared with no promised date.
  • Every generated report refreshes monthly. Only that company’s watchers receive notifications, including quiet months.
  • Keep the 24-month window and the specified initial 5-year fallback. Show changes against the previous run; the first report has no comparison.
  • The schedule supplies the next update. Failed refreshes retain the last good report, show no member-facing error, and move the date to the retry.
  • Own-company and portfolio-company reports remain within existing organization permissions.

Design proposals from the discussion

  • Interpret the 1st as the date the report becomes available, with email sent after publication. No publication hour is promised.
  • Make the operator-triggered first report available when ready, then join the first-of-month cadence.
  • Use the organization’s zone for publication and user-local formatting for actual timestamps. Label date-only schedules with their organization context. Edition names remain stable.
  • Add an edition selector, a previous-edition notice and a return-to-latest action. User-facing history extends the original v1 scope. Watch stays tied to the company across editions.

What this update does

Aligns the learning examples and a separate Paper revision with the discussion. ExampleCo’s initial report is dated 15 August, its next edition 1 September, and its next scheduled update 1 October. Illustrative transactions predate their report. The retry example uses 2 September. Exact release times and production readiness have not been validated; engineering coordination is deferred at the user’s request.
